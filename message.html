旬の食材を用いた天ぷらを主役に据え
締めに手打ち蕎麦を味わうコース仕立てで提供する一軒


落ち着いた和の空間の中
揚げたての一品一品をゆっくりとお楽しみいただけます


天ぷらは素材の持ち味を最大限に引き出す
繊細な火入れで、軽やかで上品な口当たり


季節ごとの恵みを感じながら
移ろう味わいをご堪能いただけます


コースの最後には
丹念に打ち上げた蕎麦をご用意
豊かな香りとのどごしの良さが
食事の余韻を美しく締めくくります
天ぷらと蕎麦
それぞれの魅力を引き立て合う
贅沢なひとときを
ぜひお愉しみください

Tenso Katahei
offers a course centered around seasonal tempura,
followed by handmade soba to finish the meal.


In our calm and refined Japanese setting,
you can take your time and enjoy each piece of tempura,
served freshly fried.


Our tempura is carefully prepared
to bring out the natural flavors of each ingredient,
with delicate cooking that creates a light and refined taste.


Experience the gifts of each season
and savor the subtle changes in flavor throughout the year.


To conclude the course,
we serve our carefully handmade soba.
Its rich aroma and smooth texture
bring the meal to a beautiful close.

Tempura and soba,
each enhancing the character of the other,
come together for a truly indulgent dining experience.

We invite you to savor this special moment.


Owner Chef　Takeshi Takashima
